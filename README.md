# 📊 **Business Intelligence Portfolio — John Neill**

## **Overview**
Welcome to my Business Intelligence Portfolio — a curated collection of analytics projects, semantic models, DAX libraries, and case studies that demonstrate how I design, build, and lead enterprise‑grade BI solutions.  

With 20 years of experience in data analytics, I specialize in transforming raw data into governed, scalable, and actionable insights. This portfolio highlights my approach to BI leadership: metric governance, repeatable reporting pipelines, semantic modeling, and executive‑level storytelling.

All Power BI work in this repository is built using **Power BI Desktop (free)** and published via **Power BI Public**, enabling full transparency and accessibility without requiring a corporate license.

---

## **BI Leadership Philosophy**
My philosophy centers on three pillars:

### **1. Governance First**
Effective BI begins with clarity.  
I define KPIs, KRIs, data quality rules, and semantic models before building visuals. This ensures consistency, trust, and repeatability across reporting environments.

### **2. Automation Over Manual Effort**
Analytics should scale.  
I design Power Query pipelines, parameterized refresh logic, and reusable DAX libraries that eliminate manual reporting and reduce operational overhead.

### **3. Executive Storytelling**
Dashboards must drive decisions.  
I build reports that highlight trends, risks, bottlenecks, and opportunities — enabling leaders to act quickly and confidently.
